The Sire

The first of his kind.

Stevie Rey Von is the first stallion in history to be sired by an NCHA Open Futurity Champion, win the Open Futurity himself, and go on to sire an Open Futurity Champion.

By Metallic Cat and out of Miss Ella Rey by Dual Rey, Stevie carries a pedigree engineered for power and refinement — cow sense, athletic strength, and a mind that stays honest through the longest seasons. He carried that legacy straight into the pen, and now he stamps it on a generation.

His 2015 NCHA Open Futurity Championship was the breakout. He backed it with the 2016 AQHA Junior Cutting World Championship and a 231 in the 2018 NCHA Super Stakes Open Classic finals — an arena record and a Triple Crown record in the same run. The ability was never a single moment. It was a standard.

"Everyone knows who Tom Brady is. They should also know Stevie Rey Von and his offspring." — Jason Itkin, Theorem Ranch
